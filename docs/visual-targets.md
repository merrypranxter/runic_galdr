# Visual Targets: runic_galdr

The project explores several motifs from Nordic rune magic translated into generative visuals.

- **Simple line glyphs:** Each rune is rendered using straight line segments. The minimal forms keep the shapes iconic and legible even at small sizes.
- **Bindrune overlay:** Multiple runes can be layered. Small horizontal offsets keep each glyph visible while emphasising their union.
- **Galdr resonance:** A dynamic radial interference pattern pulsates behind the rune. The frequency of the wave is mapped to a numerical representation of the rune.
- **Runic calendar wheel:** All 24 runes are arranged evenly around a circle. This wheel can animate the year’s cycle or highlight the current rune.
- **Divination spreads:** For fortune telling, random runes are drawn on screen in a spread. Hover or tap reveals their names and meanings.

These visual targets ensure the runes remain at the center of attention while adding motion and ambience.
