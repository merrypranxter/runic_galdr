# runic_galdr

> 24 runes. A generative visual grammar of the Elder Futhark. Each rune encodes a sound and concept. Bindrunes overlay multiple runes for combined meaning. Galdr visualisation turns tonal values into resonance patterns. Includes a runic calendar wheel and simple divination spreads.

**Archetype:** GRAMMAR. **Distinct from** `sephirotic_tree` (which focuses on sephirot) and `fourier_visual_synthesis` (a frequency-based visual synthesizer).

## Adjacency
- `sephirotic_tree` — both map symbolic systems to network-like visuals.
- `fourier_visual_synthesis` — harmonic synthesis inspires galdr resonances.
- `particle_systems` — dynamic flows could represent galdr energy.

## Visual Targets
- Minimalistic rune strokes drawn with straight lines.
- Bindrunes that overlay multiple glyphs with slight offsets.
- Radial resonance patterns modulated by rune frequencies.
- A runic calendar wheel placing all 24 runes around a circle.
- Random spreads of runes for divinatory layouts.

## Technical Notes
- Use WebGL or the 2D canvas for rendering.
- Data: Each rune stores line segments, phonetic value, meaning, and a numeric frequency.
- Tonal mapping: Frequencies are arbitrary values used to animate resonance patterns.
